Story: Goodbye – A Romance Short Story by Darren Jerome – Reedsy Prompts
URL: https://reedsy.com/short-story/86h7s5/

**1. 📖 SUBJECT:**  
A man prepares to leave his home and marriage behind, pausing for one last, quiet look at the remnants of their shared life before boarding his flight.

**2. 🎨 ATMOSPHERE:**  
Melancholic and wistful, tinged with bittersweet nostalgia and a quiet sense of resignation.

**3. 👶 AGE GROUP:**  
Adults (roughly 18 + – young‑adult to mature readers).

**4. 👧 WHO WOULD LOVE IT:**  
Readers who enjoy introspective, emotionally‑charged romance stories—especially those who have experienced loss, separation, or the difficulty of moving on.

**5. 🌟 MAIN LESSON:**  
Sometimes saying goodbye is painful but necessary for healing; cherishing the past doesn’t prevent us from stepping forward into the future.
